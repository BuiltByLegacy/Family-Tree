# Privacy Policy

This repository contains sensitive family-history information.

## Living people

Do not store complete birth dates, current addresses, phone numbers, personal email addresses, medical details, private legal records, or raw DNA match data.

## Minors

Store only family-approved names and relationships. Do not store schools, addresses, dates, health information, or other identifying details.

## Adoption records

Haley's Roy, Camp, and Porcello relationships must be modeled accurately, but original adoption records should remain outside GitHub unless specifically redacted and approved by Haley.

## Source documents

Keep unredacted originals in encrypted storage. Commit only redacted research copies.
