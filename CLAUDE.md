# Family Archive Working Rules

## Mission

Build a source-driven genealogy and family-history archive for Jesse Mark Lemery and Haley Ann Lemery.

## Rules

- Never invent relatives.
- Never treat a shared surname as proof.
- Separate biological, adoptive, step, legal, and social relationships.
- Cite important facts.
- Label uncertainty as Confirmed, Strongly supported, Probable, Possible, or Disproven.
- Protect living people and minors.
- Do not commit unredacted vital records or raw DNA data.
- Treat user-created family trees as leads only.
- Record conflicts instead of silently choosing one version.
