## Evidence summary

Linda's and Frank's obituaries independently identify Otto and Florence (Cummings) Schneider as their parents. Florence's own 1958 obituary (found on Find a Grave) confirms Otto as her surviving husband at that date and names all six children then living at home or nearby. No verified birth date, birthplace, parents, death date, or immigration status has yet been established for Otto himself.
