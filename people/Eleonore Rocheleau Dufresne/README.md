# Éléonore [Rocheleau / Rouleau] Dufresne

- **Status:** Living and residing in Saint-Damase 1878; status thereafter unknown
- **Evidence status:** Confirmed as Alida Dufresne's mother and Philibert Dufresne's wife, via two independent original parish records (1878 baptism, 1896 marriage). Given name Confirmed; **maiden surname disputed.**
- **Branch:** Dufresne (by marriage); maiden branch pending surname resolution

> Folder is named with the 1896-marriage spelling "Rocheleau" for continuity with earlier archive notes; this does **not** endorse it over the 1878 reading. See the conflict below.

## Identity

- **Given name: Éléonore** — **Confirmed.** The 1878 baptism of her daughter Alida (act B43) reads "Éléonore" clearly, resolving the uncertain reading ("Élionne / Élisonne / Éliesse") in the 1896 marriage record.
- **Maiden surname: DISPUTED — record both, choose neither.**

| Source | Maiden surname as written |
|---|---|
| 1878 baptism of Alida (act B43) | short name, best read **"Rouleau"** (possibly "Rousseau" / "Proulx"); clearly **not** "Rocheleau" |
| 1896 marriage of Alida (act M16) | **"Rocheleau"** |

Same given name (Éléonore) and same husband (Philibert Dufresne, who signed both records) confirm this is one woman; one record's surname was mis-transcribed. The handwriting is genuinely ambiguous in both.

## Relationships

- Husband: **Philibert Dufresne**, cultivateur of Saint-Damase — Confirmed (legitimate marriage stated in both records)
- Daughter: **Alida DuFresne Lemery (1878–1951)** — Confirmed

## Priority records

1. **Her marriage to Philibert Dufresne** (pre-1878) — the decisive source for the true maiden surname and for her own parents
2. Baptisms of Alida's **siblings** — the mother's surname should repeat and settle by consensus
3. Her own birth/baptism, and death record

## Sources

- Original baptism register: Saint-Damase parish, Saint-Hyacinthe, Québec — "Baptêmes, mariages, sépultures 1877-1899," entry **B43**, dated August 13, 1878. Digitized by FamilySearch, "Canada, Québec, Catholic Parish Registers, 1621-1979."
- Original marriage register: same collection, entry **M16**, dated October 27, 1896.
