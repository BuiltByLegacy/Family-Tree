# Florence Cummings Schneider

- **Status:** Deceased
- **Evidence status:** Strongly supported through a contemporaneous 1958 newspaper obituary (via Find a Grave); parentage of Linda and Frank confirmed
- **Branches:** Cummings, Schneider

## Vital events

- Born: approximately **1919** (age 39 at death, per obituary)
- Birthplace: **Unity, New Hampshire**
- Died: **January 21, 1958**, at Johnson Memorial Hospital, Somers, CT
- Burial: **West Cemetery, Somers, Connecticut**

## Identity

- Birth or prior surname: **Cummings**, independently reported in the obituaries of Linda and Frank Schneider, and confirmed by her own 1958 obituary

## Relationships

- Father: **Fred Cummings**
- Mother: **Lestina (Smith) Cummings**
- Spouse: Otto Schneider (survived her)
- Children named in her own 1958 obituary: three daughters — **Mrs. Clifford Richardson** (of Thompsonville), **Linda**, and **Norma** (both at home); three sons — **Frank**, **Kenneth**, and **Timothy** (all at home)
- Siblings named in her obituary: sister **Mrs. Abbe Gaudreau**; brother **Levi Cummings** (of South Portland, Maine)
- The family later included Engelman step-relatives; the parent or remarriage that created this relationship is unknown (postdates her 1958 death, so likely a later Otto remarriage)

## Evidence summary

Florence's own 1958 obituary (published in the Springfield Union, Jan. 22, 1958, transcribed on her Find a Grave memorial) independently confirms and extends what Linda's and Frank's obituaries already established: Otto and Florence (Cummings) Schneider as parents, and adds her birthplace, parents, siblings, death, and burial. This also resolves that she predeceased Otto by decades, which reframes the "Engelman stepfamily" question as most likely a later remarriage by Otto rather than Florence.

## Working timeline

- ~1919: born in Unity, New Hampshire, to Fred and Lestina (Smith) Cummings
- Before July 1942: marriage or family formation with Otto, inferred
- July 15, 1942: mother of Linda in Stafford
- July 31, 1947: mother of Frank in Stafford Springs
- January 21, 1958: died at Johnson Memorial Hospital, Somers, CT; burial West Cemetery, Somers
