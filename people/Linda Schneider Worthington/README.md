# Linda Schneider Worthington

- **Status:** Deceased
- **Evidence status:** High through detailed obituary evidence
- **Branches:** Schneider, Cummings, Worthington

## Vital events

- Born: **July 15, 1942**
- Birthplace: Stafford, Connecticut
- Died: **November 23, 2009**, reported in prior obituary research

## Relationships

- Father: Otto Schneider
- Mother: Florence Cummings Schneider (died 1958, Somers, CT, when Linda was ~15)
- Husband: Gordon R. Worthington (son of Harold Worthington and Doris Adams Worthington)
- Children: **Penny (Worthington) Lemery** (Enfield), **Bruce Worthington** (wife Maci, Enfield), **Brian Worthington** (wife Jennifer, Stafford Springs), and **Roger O. Worthington (1966–1987)**, who predeceased Linda
- Full sibling roster per Linda's own 2009 obituary: **Frank Schneider** (wife Marie, Enfield), **Kenneth Schneider** (wife Linda, AZ), **Timothy Schneider** (wife Jan, Longmeadow MA), **Norma (Schneider) Mudgett** (1944–2021; husband George, Somers), **May (Schneider) Richards** (FL) — these are full siblings via Otto and Florence. Half-siblings via Otto's remarriage after Florence's 1958 death: **Jim Engelman** (wife Theresa, Somers) and the late **Mark Engelman** (wife Kim)
- "Charlotte Schneider" is thanked separately in the obituary for care during Linda's illness — a strong but unconfirmed candidate for Otto's second wife

## Evidence summary

Linda's obituary states that she was born in Stafford to Otto and Florence (Cummings) Schneider. Frank O. Schneider's independent obituary identifies the same parents, providing strong corroboration. Florence's own 1958 obituary further corroborates the sibling set. Gordon Worthington's 2018 obituary confirms the marriage and gives Penny's married name as Lemery.
