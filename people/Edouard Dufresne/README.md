# Édouard Dufresne

- **Status:** Living 1878 (status otherwise unknown)
- **Evidence status:** Confirmed as godfather (parrain) at Alida Dufresne's 1878 baptism; **relationship to Philibert Dufresne is Possible / inferred, not proven**
- **Branch:** Dufresne (pending confirmation)

## Identity

Named as **parrain (godfather)** in the 1878 baptism of **Marie Alida Thalie Dufresne** (act B43, Saint-Damase). Recorded as "de cette paroisse."

## Relationships

- **Possible brother or close kin of Philibert Dufresne** — inference rests only on the shared surname and the godfather role, which in Québec Catholic practice often (but not always) fell to a paternal uncle. **Not proven — do not treat the shared surname as proof.**
- Godchild: **Alida DuFresne Lemery (1878–1951)**
