## Marriage — CONFIRMED via original parish register

**Married Alida Dufresne on October 27, 1896, at Saint-Damase parish, Saint-Hyacinthe, Québec** (entry M16). Original register located and transcribed directly from FamilySearch's digitized "Canada, Québec, Catholic Parish Registers, 1621-1979" collection (Saint-Damase, Baptêmes/mariages/sépultures 1877-1899).

Key details from the record:
- Married after a single publication of banns (dispensation for the other two banns granted by the Vicar General of the Diocese of St. Hyacinthe, dated October 22, 1896)
- Both were minors (under legal majority) at marriage
- Witnesses: **Paul Lussier**, described as "beau-père de l'époux" (stepfather of the groom); **Philibert Dufresne**, father of the bride; and others
- Signatures on the record: Alida Dufresne, Roël Lemery, Laura Godin, Paul Lussier, Amélie Dufresne, Elisa Dufresne, Raoul Lussier
- Officiated by M. Gauthier, Ptre

## Parents — NEW, from the marriage record

The record identifies Roel as **"fils mineur de défunt Adéiodat Lemery et de Joséphine Roy"** — minor son of the *late* Adéiodat Lemery and of Joséphine Roy, both of Saint-Damase parish.

- **Father: Adéiodat Lemery** — confirmed deceased by October 1896. (This corrects an earlier speculative guess of "Adelard Lemery" — the original record clearly reads Adéiodat.)
- **Mother: Joséphine Roy** — living at the time of the marriage. She appears to have remarried **Paul Lussier**, who signed the register as the groom's stepfather ("beau-père de l'époux"). Raoul Lussier, also a signer, may be a stepbrother from this remarriage.

This is now the priority lead for going back another generation.
