# Roger Worthington

- **Full name:** Roger O. Worthington
- **Dates:** 1966 – 1987 (per Linda Schneider Worthington's own profile, sourced from her 2009 obituary via Find a Grave)

## Known relationships
- Father: Gordon R. Worthington
- Mother: Linda Schneider Worthington
- Siblings: Penny Worthington; Bruce Worthington; Brian Worthington

## Status
Predeceased both parents. Gordon's 2018 obituary lists only Penny, Bruce, and Brian among his surviving children; Linda's own 2009 obituary independently confirms the same — listing her surviving children as "Penny Lemery, of Enfield, Bruce Worthington and his wife, Maci, of Enfield, and Brian Worthington and his wife, Jennifer, of Stafford Springs," with no mention of Roger.

## Spouse/children
**Neither parent's obituary mentions a spouse or children for Roger.** Obituaries of this kind typically do credit a predeceased child's spouse and children if any exist. The absence in both independent obituaries suggests Roger likely died without a spouse or children — consistent with dying at 20 or 21 years old (1966–1987) — though this is not a certainty.

## Evidence status
Strongly supported by two independent, cross-corroborating obituaries (Gordon's and Linda's).
