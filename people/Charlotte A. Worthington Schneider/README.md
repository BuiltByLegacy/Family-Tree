# Charlotte A. Worthington Schneider

## Vital information
- Born: September 6, 1935 · Stafford Springs, Connecticut
- Died: October 11, 2017
- Burial: Somers New West Cemetery (Rt. 190), Somers, CT

## Known relationships
- Father: Harold Worthington
- Mother: Doris Adams Worthington
- Siblings include Gordon R. Worthington and Joyce Anna Worthington
- **Husband: Otto Schneider** — her own 2017 obituary states she "passed on from this life to be with her beloved husband Otto." This confirms she is Otto's second wife, married sometime after Florence Cummings Schneider's death in 1958.
- This makes her simultaneously Gordon R. Worthington's sister and Linda Schneider Worthington's stepmother — a double connection between the Worthington and Schneider branches, on top of Gordon and Linda's own marriage.
- Possible connection to Jim and Mark Engelman, named as Linda's "brothers" in Linda's 2009 obituary: Charlotte's own obituary says she worked for years at "Engleman Convalescent Home." The near-identical spelling could be coincidence, or the Engelman surname could come from a marriage of Charlotte's that predates Otto — not yet resolved either way.
