# 1896 Roel Lemery + Alida Dufresne Marriage Record — Discovery Report

## Summary

On August 7, 2026, the family located and transcribed the original marriage register entry for **Roel Joseph Lemery** and **Alida Dufresne**, married October 27, 1896, at Saint-Damase parish, Saint-Hyacinthe, Québec. This is a primary source — the actual handwritten parish register, not a derivative index or Find a Grave entry — and it independently confirms the marriage while opening an entirely new generation of ancestors.

## Where it was found

- **Collection:** "Canada, Québec, Catholic Parish Registers, 1621-1979," FamilySearch
- **Catalog path:** Canada, Quebec, Catholic Parish Registers, 1621-1979 › Saint-Damase, Saint-Hyacinthe › Saint-Damase › Baptêmes, mariages, sépultures 1877-1899
- **Entry:** M16, dated October 27, 1896
- **Location in the digitized register:** image 671 of 801 is the "1896 St. Damase" year-divider card; the marriage entry itself is a short distance further into the register
- **FamilySearch image identifier (from the source file):** `3QS7-L999-TCF4`
- **Not name-indexed/searchable** — this register is browse-only on FamilySearch (no search hits came back for "Lemery"/"Dufresne" in this collection). It was located by manually browsing page images and using the marriage entry numbers (M-series) to triangulate the correct date range.

## Full transcription (original French)

> Le vingt-sept octobre mil huit cent quatre-vingt-seize, après la publication d'un seul ban de mariage fait au prône de la messe paroissiale entre Roël Lemery de cette paroisse, fils mineur de défunt Adéiodat Lemery et de Joséphine Roy de cette paroisse d'une part, et Alida Dufresne aussi de cette paroisse, fille mineure de Philibert Dufresne cultivateur et de [Élionne? / Élisonne?] Rocheleau de cette paroisse d'autre part, vu la dispense de deux bans accordée aux parties par Monsieur Bernard vicaire général du diocèse de St Hyacinthe, en date du vingt-deux du courant, vu le consentement des parents des époux et ne s'étant découvert aucun empêchement canonique nous prêtre curé soussigné avons reçu le mutuel consentement de mariage des époux et leur avons donné la bénédiction nuptiale en présence de Paul Lussier beau-père de l'époux, de Philibert Dufresne père de l'épouse et de plusieurs autres témoins dont les uns ont signé avec les époux —
>
> Alida Dufresne, Roël Lemery, Laura Godin, Paul Lussier, Amélie Dufresne, Elisa Dufresne, Raoul Lussier
>
> [signed] M. Gauthier, Ptre

## Translation (English)

> On October 27, 1896, after the publication of a single marriage banns made at the parish Mass, between Roël Lemery of this parish, minor son of the late Adéiodat Lemery and of Joséphine Roy of this parish, on one part, and Alida Dufresne, also of this parish, minor daughter of Philibert Dufresne, farmer, and of [Élionne?/Élisonne?] Rocheleau of this parish, on the other part — given the dispensation of two banns granted to the parties by Mr. Bernard, Vicar General of the Diocese of St. Hyacinthe, dated the 22nd of this month, and the consent of the parents of the spouses, and no canonical impediment having been discovered, we, the undersigned parish priest, received the mutual consent of marriage of the spouses and gave them the nuptial blessing, in the presence of Paul Lussier, stepfather of the groom, Philibert Dufresne, father of the bride, and several other witnesses, some of whom signed along with the spouses —
>
> Alida Dufresne, Roël Lemery, Laura Godin, Paul Lussier, Amélie Dufresne, Elisa Dufresne, Raoul Lussier
>
> [signed] M. Gauthier, Priest

## What this confirms

- The marriage date and place: October 27, 1896, Saint-Damase, Québec — now **Confirmed**, not just Find a Grave-derived
- Roel's father: **Adéiodat Lemery**, deceased by October 1896 (corrects an earlier speculative guess of "Adelard Lemery")
- Roel's mother: **Joséphine Roy**, living, likely remarried to **Paul Lussier** (who signed as "beau-père de l'époux" — stepfather of the groom)
- Alida's father: **Philibert Dufresne**, a farmer, living, personally attended and signed
- Alida's mother: surname **Rocheleau** confirmed; given name uncertain (best reading "Élionne" or "Élisonne" — needs a second opinion or a clearer source)
- A possible stepbrother connection: **Raoul Lussier**, who also signed, may be Paul Lussier's son
