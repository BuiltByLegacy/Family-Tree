# Saint-Damase Register Search — Roel Lemery (1877, negative) & Alida Dufresne (1878, found)

**Date of work:** August 8, 2026
**Register:** "Saint-Damase, Baptêmes, mariages, sépultures 1877–1899," État civil catholique, district judiciaire de Saint-Hyacinthe, Québec. Digitized by FamilySearch, "Canada, Québec, Catholic Parish Registers, 1621–1979" (browse-only). Same register that yielded the 1896 Roel × Alida marriage (act M16).

## Purpose

Test whether the two spouses' baptisms — inside the same register — independently confirm the parentage established by the single 1896 marriage record: Roel = son of Adéiodat Lemery & Joséphine Roy; Alida = daughter of Philibert Dufresne & Éléonore [___].

## Result 1 — Roel Joseph Lemery (b. reported Nov 24, 1877): NOT FOUND

The full November 1877 baptism sequence was read (leaves 23–25 of the register):

| Act | Date | Child | Parents |
|---|---|---|---|
| B71 | Nov 4 | Marie Anna Rosalie | Jacques Jodoin & Henriette Charbié |
| B72 | Nov 8 | Isidore Alfred | Joseph Dubois & Louise Deslauriers |
| B73 | Nov 12 | Jos. Albert Wilfrid Richard Lucien | Hector Beaudry & Malvina Ducharme |
| B74 | Nov 20 | Joseph Georges Eugène | Magloire Charbonneau & Léocadie Duclos |
| B75 | Nov 20 | Joseph Napoléon Augustin | Augustin Choquet & Mélina Malo dite Ayotte |
| **B76** | **Nov 25** | Napoléon Henri | Joseph Tétrault & Célina Beaudry |
| B77 | Nov 26 | Marie Célina | Tréflé Gaucher & Clarisse Blanchet |
| B78 | Nov 29 | Marie Anne Bélona | Élie Guillemette & Geneviève Choinière |

Baptisms continue B79 (Dec 6) onward with no Lemery through mid-December. Roel's reported Nov 24 birth falls between B75 (Nov 20) and B76 (Nov 25); **no act exists for him, and no Lemery appears anywhere in the November–early December 1877 sequence.**

**Conclusion:** Roel was not baptized at Saint-Damase in 1877. The register is demonstrably complete/reliable (it captured Alida's 1878 baptism on the exact reported day), so this is a true negative. His Find a Grave birthplace of **Warwick, Kent County, Rhode Island** is upgraded from *Possible* to **Probable**. The 1896 marriage's "de cette paroisse" denotes residence, not birthplace. Next: search Rhode Island (Warwick/Kent Co.) Catholic baptisms and RI civil births for late 1877; and scan the whole 1877–1899 book for *any* Lemery baptism to date the family's arrival.

## Result 2 — Alida Dufresne (b. Aug 12, 1878): FOUND — act B43

> *"Le treize août mil huit cent soixante-dix-huit, nous prêtre soussigné avons baptisé Marie Alida Thalie, née la veille, de légitime mariage de Philibert Dufresne, cultivateur, et d'Éléonore [surname disputed] de cette paroisse. Parrain Édouard Dufresne, marraine Julie Caouette, qui ont déclaré ne savoir signer. … Philibert Dufresne [signed]. J. M. Gauthier, Ptre."*

Confirms (all now **Confirmed**):
- **Birth Aug 12, 1878** ("née la veille" of the Aug 13 baptism) — matches memorial exactly.
- **Birthplace Saint-Damase.**
- **Full baptismal name Marie Alida Thalie** (marginal initials "M.A.T. Dufresne").
- **Father Philibert Dufresne, cultivateur** — signed in his own hand (consistent with his 1896 signature): a second independent source.
- New leads: godfather **Édouard Dufresne** (possible Dufresne kin), godmother **Julie Caouette**.

### Open conflict — mother's maiden surname

| Source | As written |
|---|---|
| 1878 baptism (B43) | Éléonore **[best read "Rouleau"; possibly "Rousseau"/"Proulx"; not "Rocheleau"]** |
| 1896 marriage (M16) | [Éléonore] **Rocheleau** |

Same couple; one surname mis-transcribed. Recorded, not resolved (per archive rule). Resolve via the Philibert × Éléonore marriage record or the siblings' baptisms.

## Contrast worth noting

Alida (born Saint-Damase 1878, in the register) vs. Roel (born ~1877, absent) is internally consistent: the Dufresne family was in Saint-Damase by 1878, while the Lemery–Roy family was likely elsewhere (Rhode Island) in 1877 and settled in Saint-Damase later. The two are independent families joined by the 1896 marriage.
