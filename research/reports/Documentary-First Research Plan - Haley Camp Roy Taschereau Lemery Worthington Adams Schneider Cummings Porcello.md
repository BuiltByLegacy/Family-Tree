## Privacy and archive controls

Even in a private repository, Git history is persistent. Ordinary person READMEs should not contain original or amended birth certificates, adoption decrees, case numbers, current addresses, DNA match names, kit identifiers, complete living-person birth dates, or unverified candidate identities.

| Archive layer | Appropriate contents |
|---|---|
| Genealogical profile | Historical dates, deceased ancestors, source citations, non-sensitive relationship conclusions, generalized locations |
| Restricted evidence vault outside ordinary Git history | Adoption records, unredacted certificates, complete dates for living people, court details, DNA match identities and shared-cM data, unverified Roy candidates |

## Record acquisition plan

### Haley Ann Lemery: birth and adoption records

First establish the birth jurisdiction from Haley's current amended certificate, adoption decree, hospital keepsake, passport records, school enrollment records, or family insurance documents.

If Connecticut-born, request separately:

- Current certified post-adoption certificate, to confirm legal identity, adoptive parentage, and birth jurisdiction.
- Uncertified original pre-adoption certificate, which may identify Jessica under her name at Haley's birth and may name, partially identify, or omit the biological father.

For a Connecticut original pre-adoption certificate, Haley should normally sign the special application to the town of birth. The packet requires identification and a $65 fee. An adult child or grandchild may qualify with relationship documentation; other requesters require a court order.

For adoption information, Haley should begin with Connecticut DCF form DCF-3060 and ask the Search Unit to identify the handling agency, Probate Court, case number if releasable, non-identifying social and medical history, whether the biological father was named or described, and whether contact-preference or reunion-registry forms exist. DCF-3062 should be used only if Haley deliberately chooses search or reunion participation.

### Original 1940 Worthington census image

Use indexed entries only as locators. The acceptance standard is the original NARA page.

Search Harold Worthington with spouse Doris in Somers or Tolland County, then record:

- Enumeration district, sheet, line, dwelling, and household number.
- Household relationships and every resident.
- Ages, birthplaces, education, occupation, employment, wages, home ownership or rent, and 1935 residence.
- The identity of the household informant.
- Preceding and following pages in case the household crosses a page boundary.

The probable family model includes Harold, Doris, Harold F. Jr., Donald C., Marie, Charlotte, Gordon, and Joyce, but this remains unconfirmed until the original image is reviewed.

### Harold Worthington and Doris Adams marriage

Search the Connecticut State Library Marriage Records Index, 1897–2001, under both spouses independently. Initial range: 1928–1938. Likely locations include Somers, Stafford, Enfield, and neighboring Massachusetts communities.

Candidate variants:

- Harold F. Worthington / Harold Frederick Worthington.
- Doris Adams / Doris E. Adams / Doris Eva Adams.

Once the town is found, order the full $20 certificate and extract both sets of parents, residences, occupations, birthplaces, marital status, officiant, and event location.

## Controlled research-log standard

- Repository or database.
- Exact query and filters.
- Date searched or requested.
- Result URL, image identifier, certificate number, or file number.
- Reason the result was accepted or rejected.
- Evidence grade.
- Person cards and relationship data updated.

Each acquired document should receive a full source citation. Keep the original scan unedited and create a separate exact transcription preserving spelling, capitalization, blanks, strikeouts, amendments, and illegible portions.
