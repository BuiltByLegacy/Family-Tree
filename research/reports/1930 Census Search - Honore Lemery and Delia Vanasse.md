# 1930 Census Search — Honoré Lemery and Delia Vanasse

## Status

**Result:** The Lemery–Vanasse household was **not definitively located in an original 1930 U.S. census image during this pass**.

The available evidence strongly supports that Donald H. Lemery Sr. was born in Putnam, Connecticut, on September 12, 1924, to Honora/Honoré Lemery and Delia (Vanasse) Lemery. Separate Connecticut marriage evidence also places Honoré Lemery and Delia Vanasse in Putnam by 1923. Those facts make a Putnam-area 1930 household highly plausible, but the actual census schedule still needs to be found before the household details are treated as confirmed.

## Evidence summary

### Strongly supported

- Donald H. Lemery Sr. was born September 12, 1924, in Putnam, Connecticut.
- His obituary identifies his parents as **Honora Lemery** and **Delia (Vanasse) Lemery**.
- A Connecticut marriage record/index lead places **Honore Lemery** and **Delia Vanasse** marrying in Putnam in May 1923.

### Unresolved

The 1930 census household itself remains unconfirmed. Therefore the following should **not** yet be added to person cards as census-proven facts:

- exact 1930 address
- ages of Honoré and Delia
- birthplaces and parents’ birthplaces
- immigration year or citizenship status
- occupations/industries
- age at first marriage
- home ownership/rent information
- complete child list
- enumeration sheet and household numbers

## Candidate location

Research into the 1930 enumeration-district material indicates that **Putnam City, Ward 3 (part)** was associated with **ED 17 in Windham County**. This is a search target, not proof that the family lived in that ward or ED.

The relevant 1930 Connecticut census material appears within NARA microfilm publication **T626**, with Windham County material on roll **T626-285**. The next task is to browse the actual Putnam sheets, starting with the ED associated with Ward 3 and then adjacent Putnam/Killingly districts if necessary.

The only plausible working model at present is:

- Honoré/Honora Lemery — head

**Evidence grade:** Possible / Strongly Supported family framework, but **Unresolved as a 1930 census household**.

Lorraine should not be promoted to a confirmed child of Honoré and Delia solely from this working model. Robert, Jeanette, and any other previously reported siblings also remain unconfirmed until independently linked by census, obituary, birth, marriage, cemetery, or other documentary evidence.
